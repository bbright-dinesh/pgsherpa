Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

The short version: we collect nothing. pgSherpa has no analytics, no telemetry, no accounts and no advertising. BillionBright Solutions LLP, its publisher, receives no data from the app.

What stays on your computer

Everything you set up in pgSherpa is stored only on your computer: the PostgreSQL servers you add and their connection strings, cloud provider tokens, your Anthropic API key, alert destinations, and your preferences. Uninstalling the app and removing its data folder removes all of it.

What leaves your computer, and when

pgSherpa connects only to the services below, and only for the feature named. It makes these connections from your computer directly; none of them pass through us.

Each of those services handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Ask can send the contents of query results to Anthropic; do not use it on data you may not share with them.

What pgSherpa can do to your databases

Nothing that changes them, unless you ask. Every statement runs in a read-only transaction. A statement that writes, and cancelling or terminating a session, are only sent after you switch the SQL box to write, which macOS confirms with Touch ID or your password, and each still needs you to type or confirm it. Nothing is ever changed at a cloud provider.

Children

pgSherpa is a tool for database professionals and is not directed at children.

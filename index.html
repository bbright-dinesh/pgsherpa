A PostgreSQL companion that sits on your desktop.

pgSherpa is a small mountain guide who watches your PostgreSQL servers from the corner of your screen and tells you, in plain words, when something needs you.

  • Health. Locks, long queries, replication, vacuum, wraparound, connections: one card per problem, with the rows behind it.
  • Security. Your version against the latest release and known CVEs, and configuration worth fixing.
  • Cloud. Backups, failovers, maintenance and replicas on DigitalOcean and Supabase.
  • Read-only SQL. A box beside him with PostgreSQL completion; results you can sort, filter and copy.
  • Ask. Questions about your database, answered with the Claude API using your own key.

pgSherpa only reads. Every statement runs in a read-only transaction, and it never changes anything at a cloud provider.

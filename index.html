A PostgreSQL companion that sits on your desktop.

pgSherpa is a small mountain guide who watches your PostgreSQL servers from the corner of your screen and tells you, in plain words, when something needs you.

  • Health. Locks, long queries, replication, vacuum, wraparound, connections: one card per problem, with the rows behind it.
  • Security. Your version against the latest release and known CVEs, and configuration worth fixing.
  • Cloud. Backups, failovers, maintenance and replicas on AWS, DigitalOcean and Supabase.
  • SQL, read-only by default. A box beside him with PostgreSQL completion; results you can sort, filter and copy. To write, click its read > prompt and confirm with Touch ID or your password.
  • Ask. Questions about your database, answered with the Claude API using your own key.

pgSherpa reads by default. Every statement runs in a read-only transaction unless you switch the SQL box to write, which asks for Touch ID or your password each time the app starts. It never changes anything at a cloud provider.

Get it

Download pgSherpa 1.2.1 for macOS (signed and notarised by Apple). Open the file and drag pgSherpa to Applications. All versions.

It is also on its way to the Mac App Store. This page will link to it when it is available.

Requires macOS 12 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
